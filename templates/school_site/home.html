WELCOME TO NGUNDU HALT

A school community built around possibility.

Ngundu Halt Government High School serves learners and families in Chivi District, Masvingo Province.

Our website is a place for families to learn about the school, find academic information, follow news and events, and connect with the wider school community. Our motto, “Sweat & Sacrifice — Produce Success,” speaks to the value of effort, perseverance and shared responsibility.
